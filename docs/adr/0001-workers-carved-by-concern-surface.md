# Workers carved by concern surface

The Assistant routes to three Workers carved by *what kind of concern they own* — `payments` (mutations: charge, refund), `risk` (assessment + blocking), `support` (read-only data) — rather than by payment rail (card / bank transfer / wallet) or by lifecycle operation (charge / refund / dispute / subscription). This carving is chosen specifically because it produces clear tool-surface boundaries (mutate vs assess vs read) and naturally generates ambiguous queries that meaningfully stress the Assistant's routing — e.g. *"there's a $4,000 charge from Belarus, refund it"* requires `risk` then `payments` and is unscoreable under a rail-based or single-axis carving. Rail-based routing would be too easy for the LLM (keyword pattern-matching wins); pure lifecycle routing has too much overlap (refunds inside subscriptions); both produce weaker benchmark signal.
